About This Platform

This platform helps spread awareness about government schemes designed to reduce dropout rates, improve access to education, and support students from disadvantaged backgrounds. You can explore scholarships, support programs, financial aid, and welfare schemes for school and college students.

Key Government Schemes to Prevent Dropout

🎒 Mid-Day Meal Scheme

Provides free nutritious meals to school students (Class 1 to 8) to boost attendance, health, and reduce dropout rates.

📘 Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan (SSA)

A holistic program integrating school education from pre-primary to Class 12, funding free uniforms, textbooks, remedial classes, and school infrastructure.

💰 National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS)

Scholarships for meritorious students from economically weaker sections to prevent dropout at Class 8 & beyond.

🎓 PM Scholarship for College Students

Financial support for higher education, especially for underprivileged youth to prevent college dropout due to economic stress.

👧 Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (Education Support for Girls)

Encourages girls’ education through awareness, counseling support, and scholarships to prevent dropout among female students.

🏫 Free Uniforms & Textbooks Program

Under RTE Act, students up to Class 8 receive free textbooks & uniforms, reducing financial burden and preventing dropout.

🚌 Transport/Hostel Facility Scheme

Supports students from remote rural areas with free or subsidized transport and hostel facilities.

How These Schemes Reduce School Dropout

✔ Financial support reduces the economic burden on families
✔ Scholarships encourage academic continuation
✔ Free meals improve attendance and health
✔ Girl-centric schemes reduce gender-based dropout
✔ Hostel & transport support helps students in remote areas
