🤝 How Mentor Assignment Works

1. Students send a mentor request

They choose the type of help they need (academic, emotional, career, etc.).

2. Mentors apply to volunteer

Each mentor lists their expertise and availability.

3. The platform/system matches them

  • Based on expertise
  • Student's challenge area
  • Availability
  • Language preference

4. Admin or automated system assigns the mentor

Once a suitable match is found, the student receives mentor details and can begin guidance sessions.

Matching Example

Student need: Academic Support → struggling in math

Best mentor: Mentor with tutoring experience in math

Result: Student receives personalized help → reduced dropout risk
